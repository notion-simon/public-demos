# Grandmother's House Eats the Guests - Final Visual Polish Spec

## Core Mechanic
The game keeps one simple verb: **click a room to rotate its hallway clockwise**.

## Gameplay
Multiple guests move through a 4x4 haunted dollhouse at the same time. Green/☕ guests want the Tea Exit; red/🦷 guests want the Mouth Exit. Guests follow matching hallway openings. Stuck, impatient, or wrongly routed guests raise suspicion. Route enough guests correctly before suspicion reaches 100% to win.

## Visual Polish Goals Implemented
- Richer haunted-house mood with vignette, moon glow, smoke, candlelight, shadows, and a roof/chimney silhouette.
- Rooms are more distinct through color, wallpaper texture, labels, and subtle room-specific props/icons.
- Hallways are bright golden shapes with glow, highlights, and clean shared-edge connection glints.
- Rotation has a stronger feel: room pulse, spark particles, brief spin trail, and slight screen shake.
- Guests are more expressive with face details, colored aura, target icons, urgency indicator, and bobbing movement.
- Tea and mouth exits are more theatrical: steaming teacup and animated chomping mouth.
- Success, wrong route, stuck guest, win, and loss feedback use brighter floating text, particles, glow, shake, and audio cues.

## Constraints
Static HTML/CSS/JS only. No external assets, no server components, no multiplayer.
